Every pill here has exactly the same active ingredient.

i.

Same chemical, same dose, same form.

Paracetamol 500 mg is paracetamol 500 mg. Bangladesh’s drug regulator holds every registered brand to the same standard. In this dataset alone, 74 companies sell this exact tablet.

ii.

Yet the label decides what you pay.

The cheapest listed brand costs ৳0.35 a tablet. The most expensive costs ৳1.30, nearly four times more for an identical molecule. For a cough syrup it’s small change. For medicine you take every day for life, it adds up to rent money.

iii.

And nobody shows you the list.

Prescriptions name a brand, not the chemical. Shelves show one or two options. Most patients never learn a cheaper equal exists, and when their brand runs out they assume the medicine is gone.

iv.

Sashroy shows you all of it.

Search the brand on your prescription or the chemical printed on the strip. You get every equivalent, matched by generic, strength and form, ranked by price per tablet, ml or gram. Pick the one that fits your pocket, with your doctor’s OK.

Chapter three · What’s broken

Four habits that keep
prices in the dark.

These aren’t one company’s fault. They’re how the whole market works today, and every patient pays for them.

Hidden prices

Prices aren’t shown side by side.

Each brand’s price is printed on its own box. To compare, you’d have to see every box of every brand at once, and no shop shelf holds all 74 paracetamols.

What we do: one list, every equivalent brand, cheapest first, priced per unit so bottle sizes and box sizes compare fairly.

Brand-only prescribing

The prescription names a brand, not the chemical.

When only a brand name is written, patients take it as the only option. Brand promotion aimed at prescribers rewards that habit. The patient pays for it at the counter.

What we do: search the prescribed brand and see its generic name plus every equal alternative. Then ask your doctor or pharmacist whether you can switch.

Margin-led selling

The counter sells what pays best.

When a shop chooses which equivalent to hand over, the one with the better margin often wins. You can’t push back on a choice you can’t see.

What we do: you choose the brand. The cart even flags a cheaper equal for each item before you pay.

“Out of stock”

A missing brand looks like a missing medicine.

Supply gaps hit one brand at a time. Without a list of equivalents, an empty shelf looks like a dead end, and people skip doses or pay black-market prices.

What we do: when a brand is out, in-stock equivalents are shown right away.

Questions

Asked, answered.

Is a cheaper brand really the same medicine?

We only call two products equivalent when they have the same generic (active ingredient), the same strength and the same dosage form. Extended- and delayed-release versions are kept separate. Fillers and coatings can differ, so always ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching.

Where do the prices come from?

Our catalogue is built from an open, MedEx-derived dataset of medicines sold in Bangladesh. Prices change and the snapshot can contain errors, so prices that look wrong are marked “price unverified” and left out of savings claims. The pharmacy confirms the final price on your order.

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can check out as a guest and track your order with its ID and your phone number. Creating an account (email or Google) saves your addresses and order history.

Do you deliver outside Dhaka?

Yes. The checkout covers all 8 divisions, 64 districts and their upazilas, with a lower delivery fee inside Dhaka and free delivery above a set order value.

Why is the Android app an APK and not on the Play Store?

It’s a test build so you can try the app today. Play Store and App Store releases come next. Each test build replaces the last, so if an update won’t install, uninstall the old version first.

Is this medical advice?

No. Sashroy is a price comparison and ordering service. Follow your doctor’s prescription and ask a pharmacist before changing any medicine.
